Filament Re-wind Machine

Design, FEA validation and manufacture of a high-reliability filament re-wind system.

Overview

This project focused on the design and manufacture of a 3D printer filament re-wind machine that slashed material costs by roughly 60% by transferring bulk, third-party filament onto proprietary, Sindoh-compatible spools. Driven by a single AC induction motor and a 120:1 reduction gearhead, the system simultaneously ran spool rotation, a reciprocating filament guide, and a spring-loaded tension control mechanism.

Engineering & Compliance

To ensure complete operational reliability and compliance with EU Machinery Regulations, critical components like the worm gears and cantilevered spool shafts were structurally validated using ANSYS finite element analysis (FEA) alongside physical prototyping.

Control & Accessibility

Complementing the machine was an inclusive, height-adjustable mobile workbench designed to hold four full and four empty spools while housing a standard electrical enclosure. The station integrated an automated low-voltage control suite—managed by an Arduino and a micro:bit—which featured an ultrasonic sensor to auto-stop the motor when a spool was full, electronic safety switches, color-coded LED indicators, and voice-activated height adjustments to maximize accessibility for all operators.
